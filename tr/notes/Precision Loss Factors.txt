Loss of precision of measurements in strain gauges are often as a result of:

-Offset drift : a slow unwanted change in an electronic circuit's output voltage or current over time, even when the input remains constant
-1/f noise : a type of electronic noise where the noise power is inversely proportional to frequency
-line noise : EM noise that distorts signals in data transmissions or electrical circuits. It is caused by:
	- EMI
	- Impedance mismatching
	- Power Line fluctuations

There are some solutions to minimise the effect of these sources of error. One solution is to use an ac signal to excite the bridge.


Note:
A parameter of amplifiers that quantify their ability to reject signals that appear identically on both inputs (e.g noise) is called CMRR (Common-Mode Rejection Ratio)
