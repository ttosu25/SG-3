The system seems to have a data flow structure as follows:

Force -> Foil Grid -> Piezoelectric signals -> Instrumentation Amplifier -> Signal Conditioning -> Digitization -> strain calculation/data logging system.

A reference voltage must be supplied to the instrumentation amplifier because strain gauges
can increase and decrease in resistance which produces a positive and negative differential bridge voltage. 

The reference voltage should be half the power supply voltage. A reference voltage at mid supply biases the output voltage of instrumentation amplifier to allow differential measurements in the positive and negative direction.
